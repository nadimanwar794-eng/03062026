---
name: Lucent lesson navigation
description: Navigation rule for Competition Lucent subjects and admin-added lessons
---

Competition Lucent subject selection must always show the subject's lesson list before opening page-wise notes. This applies even when there is only one lesson, so a later admin-added lesson remains discoverable in the same place.

**Why:** Skipping the lesson list for a single lesson caused students to land directly on notes, and the subsequent lesson-list state could render as an empty screen after content changed.

**How to apply:** Keep the lesson list driven by the current Lucent notes data. A lesson card should be the only path from the subject list into its page list or MCQ viewer; keep this rule scoped to Competition Lucent so school lesson flows are unaffected.
